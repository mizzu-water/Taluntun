Cavite College Scholarships

Discover verified scholarships, government subsidies, and private foundation grants available to students studying in Dasmariñas and across Cavite.

National Gov

DOST-SEI S&T Scholarship

Prestigious undergraduate scholarship for top STEM and non-STEM students enrolling in priority science, mathematics, computing, and engineering degrees.

Coverage: Tuition subsidy, ₱7,000/mo stipend, book allowance
Applicable in: CvSU, TUP-Cavite, DLSU-D (accredited programs)

National Gov

CHED Scholarship Program (CSP)

Direct financial grant for qualified graduating senior high school students enrolling in CHED-identified priority higher education degree programs.

Coverage: Up to ₱120,000/year for Full Merit beneficiaries
Eligibility: General Weighted Average of 93% or higher

Free Tuition Act

UNIFAST Free Higher Education

Republic Act 10931 guarantees 100% free tuition and miscellaneous fees for all eligible Filipino students in State and Local Universities in Cavite.

Coverage: 100% Tuition & miscellaneous institutional fees
Participating SUCs: CvSU Main/Campuses, TUP-Cavite

Cavite Provincial

Provincial Government of Cavite (PGC)

Provincial educational assistance established to support bonafide Caviteño residents pursuing baccalaureate degrees in accredited higher education institutions.

Coverage: Semester financial subsidy and academic incentives
Eligibility: Cavite residency certificate & voter's certification
Visit Cavite.gov.ph →
Dasma City LGU

Dasmariñas City Educational Assistance

City scholarship programs and full subsidized college enrollment through Kolehiyo ng Lungsod ng Dasmariñas (KLD) for registered Dasmariño youth.

Coverage: 100% Free Tuition at KLD + City stipend programs
Target: Midwifery, Nursing, IT, and Psychology students
Inquire at Dasma LGU →
