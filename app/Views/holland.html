The Holland Codes (RIASEC)

Dr. John Holland's theory posits that people and work environments can be loosely classified into six different groups. Discover which traits resonate with your personality.

R

Realistic (The Doer)

Practical, visual, and hands-on tools, machinery, athletics, nature, and tangible physical objects.

I

Investigative (The Thinker)

Analytical, intellectual, and scientific observing, analyzing, problem-solving, and interpreting data.

A

Artistic (The Creator)

Creative, original, independent self-expression, graphic design, writing, music, performing arts.

S

Social (The Helper)

Empathetic, communicative individuals. Enjoys teaching, mentoring, healing, counseling.

E

Enterprising (The Persuader)

Ambitious, energetic leaders who enjoy entrepreneurship, business tactics, negotiation, marketing.

C

Conventional (The Organizer)

Organized, systematic, detail-oriented managing data structures, accounting ledgers, office logistics.
