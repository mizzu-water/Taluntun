The Holland Codes (RIASEC)

Dr. John L. Holland's theory posits that individuals and occupational environments can be categorized into six broad types. Finding harmony between your primary traits and your college major leads to higher career satisfaction and academic excellence.

R
The Doer

Realistic

Practical, hands-on, and tool-oriented. Realistic individuals thrive when working with machinery, mechanical structures, physical tools, and the natural outdoors.

Top Cavite Degrees:

BS Civil Engineering, BS Mechanical Engineering, BS Architecture, BS Information Technology

Sample Careers:

Structural Engineer, Architect, Network Hardware Tech, Robotics Specialist

I
The Thinker

Investigative

Analytical, intellectual, and scientific. Investigative minds love untangling mathematical puzzles, analyzing raw data, and conducting scientific research.

Top Cavite Degrees:

BS Computer Science, BS Medical Technology, BS Applied Mathematics, BS Biology

Sample Careers:

Software Architect, Data Scientist, Clinical Researcher, Biochemist

Browse Investigative Degrees →
A
The Creator

Artistic

Creative, original, and independent. Creators seek expressive outlets across visual communication, interactive storytelling, digital illustration, and UX design.

Top Cavite Degrees:

BA Multimedia Arts, BS Digital Animation, BA Communication, BS Interior Design

Sample Careers:

Product Designer, Graphic Artist, Animator, Creative Director

Browse Artistic Degrees →
S
The Helper

Social

Empathetic, communicative, and compassionate. Social individuals find profound meaning in teaching, healing, counseling, and nurturing human development.

Top Cavite Degrees:

BS Nursing, Bachelor of Secondary Education, BS Psychology, BS Physical Therapy

Sample Careers:

Registered Nurse, Secondary Teacher, Guidance Counselor, Physical Therapist

Browse Social Degrees →
E
The Persuader

Enterprising

Ambitious, charismatic, and strategic. Persuaders excel at commercial leadership, corporate negotiation, public relations, and organizing teams.

Top Cavite Degrees:

BS Business Administration, BS Marketing Management, BS Entrepreneurship, BS Hospitality

Sample Careers:

Marketing Manager, Venture Founder, Corporate Sales Lead, Operations Director

C
The Organizer

Conventional

Organized, detail-oriented, and systematic. Organizers enjoy rigorous record-keeping, financial audits, database management, and structured procedures.

Top Cavite Degrees:

BS Accountancy, BS Management Accounting, BS Information Systems, BS Customs Admin

Sample Careers:

Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Internal Auditor, Systems Admin, Financial Analyst
