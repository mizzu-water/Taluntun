Theoretical Foundation

The Holland Hexagonal Model

According to John Holland's hexagonal model, traits adjacent to each other share higher psychological congruence. For example, Investigative (I) naturally harmonizes with Realistic (R) for engineering, and Artistic (A) for innovative software design.

R • Realistic
The Doer

Enjoys tactile machines, athletic systems, structural tools, and physical construction.

I • Investigative
The Thinker

Excels in abstract problem-solving, laboratory research, scientific inquiry, and logic.

A • Artistic
The Creator

Values self-expression, graphic communication, musical or linguistic creativity, and UX aesthetics.

S • Social
The Helper

Driven by empathy, community mentorship, patient care, educational teaching, and social impact.

E • Enterprising
The Persuader

Enjoys startup entrepreneurship, sales negotiation, strategic management, and project leadership.

C • Conventional
The Organizer

Takes pride in precision auditing, database architecture, financial records, and operational rigor.
