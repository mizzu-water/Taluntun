Discover Your Holland Code

The RIASEC model breaks down personalities into six distinct career types. By understanding your unique code, you unlock the secret to finding a career that naturally fits your strengths and interests.

Read about the 6 types

Realistic

The Doers

Practical, hands-on, and tool-oriented. They prefer working with things rather than people.

Investigative

The Thinkers

Analytical, intellectual, and scientific. They love solving complex problems through research.

Artistic

The Creators

Creative, original, and expressive. They thrive in environments where they can design and innovate.

Social

The Helpers

Empathetic, friendly, and cooperative. They find fulfillment in teaching, healing, and guiding others.

Enterprising

The Persuaders

Ambitious, outgoing, and energetic. They excel in leadership, sales, and managing teams.

Conventional

The Organizers

Detail-oriented, systematic, and efficient. They are highly organized and work well with data.
